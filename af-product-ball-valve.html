Ball Valve Manufacturer and Stockist

Product Overview

Quarter-turn valve providing reliable shutoff with a low-resistance flow path. India Steel Industries lists this product within its industrial piping and process product range. The product is intended to be selected according to material grade, dimensions, pressure class, connection type and service conditions.

More Details of the Product

This product is suitable for professional piping, fabrication and maintenance environments where dimensional accuracy, dependable connections and service compatibility are important. Final configuration can be specified to match project drawings, applicable standards and operating conditions.

Specifications

  • Body materials: Cast, forged or gun-metal construction depending on model
  • Connection: Flanged, threaded, socket-weld or butt-weld as applicable
  • Service: Isolation, non-return, throttling or fine control
  • Pressure / temperature: Selected to suit the service duty
