Butt Weld Pipe Fittings Manufacturer and Stockist

Product Overview

Butt-weld fittings for permanent, high-integrity piping connections. India Steel Industries lists this product within its industrial piping and process product range. The product is intended to be selected according to material grade, dimensions, pressure class, connection type and service conditions.

More Details of the Product

This product is suitable for professional piping, fabrication and maintenance environments where dimensional accuracy, dependable connections and service compatibility are important. Final configuration can be specified to match project drawings, applicable standards and operating conditions.

Specifications

  • Material: Carbon Steel, Stainless Steel, Alloy Steel and project-specified grades
  • Typical standards: ASME / ASTM / ANSI / MSS / DIN as applicable
  • Configuration: Butt-weld, socket-weld, threaded or fabricated depending on product
  • Size: Available in standard and project-specific sizes

Applications

  • Oil & Gas
