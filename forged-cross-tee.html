Forged Cross Tee Manufacturer and Stockist

What is Forged Cross Tee?

Forged Cross Tees connect four pipe runs from a single forged body, commonly used where a pipeline needs to branch in two opposite directions simultaneously. India Steel Industries manufactures Forged Cross Tees as per ASME B16.11 in socket weld and threaded end connections.

More Details of the Product

India Steel Industries is one of the leading Manufacturer and Stockist of Forged Cross Tee in Kolkata, West Bengal, Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Assam, Andhra Pradesh, Hyderabad, Telangana, Chennai, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, and Ahmedabad, Gujarat. Our one-piece forged construction offers higher pressure integrity, commonly used in gauge and instrument manifold piping at competitive prices.

Specifications of Forged Cross Tee

  • Standard: Carbon Steel ASME B16.11 Cross Tee | Stainless Steel ASME B16.11 Cross Tee
  • Size: 1/4" NB TO 4" NB
  • Class: 3000 LBS, 6000 LBS
  • Type: Forged Cross Tee, Reducing Cross Tee
  • Form: Socket Weld, Threaded (NPT/BSP)
  • Value Added Services: Hot Dipped Galvanizing, Electro Polish, Threading
  • Production Grade: Carbon Steel, Stainless Steel, Duplex Steel
